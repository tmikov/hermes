emitSafeStoreOrSlow declines an old-target store to the helper whenever
the old-generation marking barriers are on or a compactee exists (the
code's steps (2) and (3): x86-64 JitEmitter-internal.cpp:565-584, arm64
:585-603; steps 3-4 in the spec's list). With boxing inline (spec
2026-10-29-jit-inline-boxed-double-store-design.md), these are what is
left of NavierStokes' PutByVal helper calls under HV32 (x86-64 Release,
instrumented run, 2026-10-29): 38134941 helper calls against 52693843
inline stores, so 42.0% of its PutByVal stores still take the helper.
All of them but 190 young-generation-full fallbacks are these declines:
30579841 boxing stores with marking on and 3597000 with a compactee
(37.6% of the stores), plus 2989716 and 968194 stores of values that
need no box. Crypto has none of them: of its 1875827 remaining helper
calls (1.0% of its PutByVal stores), 638 are young-generation-full
fallbacks and the other 1875189 fail the bounds and hole guards. The
NavierStokes profile still spends 17.4% of its samples in
putByValWithReceiver_RJS and ArrayImpl::_setOwnIndexedImpl /
_haveOwnIndexedImpl, and 13.8% in HadesGC. The spec named this a
separate change, measured after the box arm.
